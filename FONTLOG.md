# Sisifo FONTLOG

Sisifo is a modified version of **Ortica Linear** by Benedetta Bovani and Collletttivo (SIL OFL 1.1),
taken from https://github.com/collletttivo/ortica at commit 27bf44d (sources `source/Ortica Linear-{Light,Regular,Bold}.ufo`).
It is made for SisyphusFit, an offline workout tracker: plain Ortica Linear where text is small, carved where it is
large. Sisifo is not Ortica and is not endorsed by its authors.

## 0.1 (2026-10-22), stage 1: variable Ortica
- Renamed to Sisifo; copyright lines kept and Sisifo's added.
- Masters made compatible (25 glyphs): Light and Regular curves split where Bold has extra points, at the same
  arc-length fractions (shapes unchanged); f_k contours reordered; zero.dnom, empty in Ortica's Regular, drawn by
  interpolating Light and Bold (a helper glyph; not exported).
- Combining marks set to zero width, as in Ortica's exported fonts (Glyphs does this at export; fontmake does not).
- Variable font, wght 300-700 (Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold), 88 KB.
- Gate against the original Ortica Linear OTFs: 394 glyphs at 300, 400 and 700 match (advance equal, merged-overlap
  area within 1 %, bounds within 3 units); no outline errors or direction flips at 300-700; kerning-shaped widths equal.
- Specimen at the 6T density (build/specimen.png): 300, 400 and 700 look identical to the originals; 500 and 600 sit
  evenly between.

## 0.2 (2026-10-22), stage 2: figures
- Tabular lining figures zero.tf ... nine.tf, each one component of its digit, in one advance per master: widest digit
  ink + 2 x the median of the smaller sidebearings (Light 611, Regular 626, Bold 656; 641 at 550). Each digit keeps
  its own left/right balance inside the cell. `tnum` maps the ten lining digits; default digits are unchanged.
- Oldstyle digits stay proportional: with `onum` and `tnum` both on, `onum` wins.
- Gate: Ortica's 394 glyphs still match at 300/400/700; the ten tabular digits share one width at 300, 400, 550 and
  700; "0000" ... "9999" set with tnum by HarfBuzz spread 0.000 px at 100 px (without tnum they spread, as a control).
- Numbers axis (tools/measure.py): Ortica 0.639, Sisifo 0.894 (tabular 0 -> 1). Still weakest: the decimal point,
  0.58 (its ink against the stroke of "1"). 89 KB.

## 0.3 (2026-10-22), stage 3: carve variants (owner review pending)
- `CARV` axis 0-100 (0 = Ortica Linear, 100 = carved), generated by tools/carve.py from the plain sources. Curves are
  split at the same turn fractions in all six masters. CARV 100 straightens the pieces into facets and lines up
  flats at smooth extremes; overshoots snap onto the baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender or descender.
- Rules found in the build: no cut deeper than half the master's thin stroke (OE's diagonal cut through it);
  overlapped (hidden) pieces keep their curve (m's arch came loose); snapping only for an overshoot, in the ink's
  direction (counters and the I's serif underside were pulled onto lines). The cent bars now run 12 units into the c
  (tools/joins.py; the plain outline is unchanged).
- Five variants (A-E), each 152-234 KB, all pass the gate: Ortica's 394 glyphs at CARV 0, no outline errors or
  direction flips at CARV 0/50/100, topology unchanged when carved. Cubic-to-quadratic error 0.25 unit.
- Scores (docs/results/stage3-*.json; eight axes, faithful at half weight): B 0.838, C 0.832, D 0.828 (a tie),
  A 0.813, E 0.765. The blind panel's hidden control (plain) scored stone 1, carving 0 in every vote.
- Round 2 (owner: "even in A, the font still lost personality"). Measured cause: round 1's A cut 23 round glyphs
  about 500 times at 30 degrees; Ortica Angular Bold cuts them 369 times at 42, and cuts the two sides of a stroke
  out of phase. New variants:
  - R / RL, chiselled rules: 42-degree cuts; a counter's extremes stay corners while the outside is flat (decided
    locally: ink away from the curve's centre); each cut capped at half the stroke it cuts into, by a ray cast.
  - G / GL, Ortica Angular's own cuts (tools/angular_fit.py): Angular Bold's corners projected onto Linear Bold
    (contours matched by whole bounding box; nearest point, or share of length when that breaks order); CARV 100
    Bold is Angular's shape exactly (overlap 1.00); lighter weights take Bold's moves with the across-stroke part
    scaled by their own stroke thickness. 150 glyphs fit in G, 154 in GL; the rest (A, R, q, 6 among them) use the
    rules, each with its reason in docs/results/stage3-round2-fit-*.json.
  - RL and GL carve Light at 0.5 and Regular at 0.75 of the full carve.
  All four pass the gate. Nine axes with a blind personality axis (references: plain, and Angular Bold):
  GL 0.848, G 0.827, RL 0.814, R 0.810, B 0.753. Personality GL 0.92 (Angular itself 3.7 of 4); carving is
  stronger in the rules (0.75) than in Angular's cuts (0.50-0.58: lopsided 3 and 8, skewed S, uneven facets).
- Round 3 (owner: guided fits for the 6 and the other fallbacks, even out 3, 8, S; re-score GL):
  - A general fit (angular_fit.plan_general) for glyphs the first fit could not take: Angular merged to its
    visible outline, corners attached to the nearest visible Linear point; between corners that are neighbours on
    Angular's outline points go onto the edge, elsewhere (inside an overlap) they move with the corners. Adds A, AE,
    R, q, @, (R), pilcrow and the f-ligatures: GL now fits 170 glyphs; Eng, Thorn, perthousand (components),
    ae and foursuperior stay on the rules.
  - The 6 by hand anchors (carve.ANCHORS): Angular's inner-arm and hook corners pinned along Linear's inner arm
    and terminal; overlap with Angular's 6 at Bold 0.989, where its arm had been one straight cut.
  - 3, 8, S evened (angular_fit.even): Angular's facet corners re-spaced at equal steps along its own edge between
    sharp corners. Facet-length spread 0.15 -> 0.02 (3), 0.39 -> 0.08 (8), 0.51 -> 0.04 (S). Smoothing (Taubin)
    was tried first and made the S worse (turn spread 10.5 -> 17.0); the lean measure showed no lean to fix.
  - Gate passes; 137 tests. Blind re-score, new GL beside round 2's GL: 0.828 each, every axis equal. The same
    round 2 image scored 0.848 in round 2, so the panel cannot resolve differences under about 0.02.

## 0.4 (2026-10-23), stage 4: inscriptional capitals (owner review pending)
- `ss01`: A-Z plus every accented capital built from components (accents re-centred over the new capital; marks
  below seated by boolean check: overlapping, no loose part, no pinhole). Built in memory at build time
  (tools/ss01.py); default glyphs untouched.
- Two sources, both carved: GLP, Sisifo's own carved capitals re-proportioned to Cinzel's measured widths in bands
  that keep stems; GLZ, Cinzel's capitals (SIL OFL 1.1, Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors) at the weight
  whose I stem matches Sisifo's (Light extrapolated), carved by the rules.
- Both pass the gate. Blind panel, ten axes with inscription: GLZ 0.868, GL (release, no ss01) 0.811, GLP 0.767.
  GLP read as condensed: Sisifo's O, C, D are already about as wide as Cinzel's (1.02-1.05), so it mainly narrowed
  E, S, B. GLZ has no kerning for its capitals yet.

## 0.5 (2026-10-23): release GLZ
- The release (`python tools/build.py release` -> fonts/variable/Sisifo[CARV,wght].ttf, 244 KB) is GLZ: the GL carve
  with Cinzel's capitals as ss01. With ss01 off it is GL glyph for glyph (all 405 glyphs, outlines and advances, at
  12 weight/carve points). OFL.txt and the font's copyright credit Cinzel.
- Self-contained: Ortica (sources and published fonts, commit 27bf44d) and Cinzel are in vendor/ with their
  licences; the font search's measure script is tools/search_measure.py; requirements.txt pins the build tools.
- GL is not a separate file: `ss01` off is GL glyph for glyph, now pinned by a test. README.md documents the axes,
  the features and the build.
- ss01 kerning (2026-10-23): Cinzel's own kern feature, read at each master's Cinzel weight (Light extrapolated)
  and scaled to Sisifo's cap height, between the ss01 capitals (one group per letter with its accented forms) and
  against punctuation: 653 pairs per master (A-V -110, T-A -78, L-T -100, T-period -85 at Regular). Checked by eye
  (AVATAR, TAVOLA, WAVY, L'AVVENTO, T.A.V.: the gaps close). Two optical-spacing numbers were tried and both misjudge
  diagonal pairs (one saturates, one penalises the tight AV a designer wants), so neither is used. Release 255 KB.
